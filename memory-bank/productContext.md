# Product Context: ISICECAKEOISIS INC.

## Problem Domain & Purpose
- "The Screen Was the Bottleneck. Agents Are the Market."
- Autonomous software agents are superseding manual human DOM navigation. Companies must become machine-legible through deterministic APIs, MCP servers, Skills, and structured `/llms.txt` manifests.
- Unifies on-device AI personality modeling (Yuli) with physical reputation hardware (Aevloc NFC stands), chemical consumer harm-reduction kits (Purple Canary), and local-first knowledge systems (CaffeineMD).

## Key User & Agent Flows
- **Cinematic Snap-Scroll Deck**: 9 vertical viewports with smooth-snap scrolling, side dot navigation, keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+M, Esc, Arrows), and slide indicator.
- **System Directory Modal**: Interactive overview of all 9 viewports, quick directives (Request materials, copy manifest, toggle low-power mode), and 6 sibling hub access links.
- **Accredited Investor Inquiries**: Dedicated modal funnel with validation, scope selection, securities compliance notice, and routing to TehWiz Productions.
- **Machine Legibility**: Dedicated terminal card with copyable `/llms.txt` manifest and server-level `/llms.txt` raw route.
- **Low-Power Mode**: Toggleable static/reduced-motion mode for 60fps battery optimization.
